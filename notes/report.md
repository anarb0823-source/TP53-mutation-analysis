TP53 Frameshift Mutation Analysis Report

TP53 is a gene that produces p53 protein that protects our gene from getting attacked by cancers. 
It acts like tumor suppresser.
Regulates cell divison by keeping cells from growing and dividing too many continuesly.
The frameshift mutation in this gene can change its whole coding sequence and make it lose it's ability to function. 

Based off of many studies on patient with cancer it showed that most of the time , cancer mutation has bypass this gene to create its tumor.
